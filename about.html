About the shop

Good barbering starts with people.

BEXAR COUNTY KUTZ is a San Antonio neighborhood barbershop built around fresh looks, careful detail, and a welcoming chair.

A good cut is personal.
Our approach

A place to reset and leave feeling sharp.

BEXAR COUNTY KUTZ serves customers at 5404 Military Dr W in San Antonio. Public listings describe a barbershop offering cuts, fades, tapers, beard trims and line-ups, with customers visiting for adult and kids' haircuts. Let your barber know what you want, bring a reference photo if you have one, and discuss the finish before the cut begins.

The business story and team biographies were not available in the public information reviewed, so this page focuses on the shop's location, listed services and the experience customers can expect when discussing a haircut.
